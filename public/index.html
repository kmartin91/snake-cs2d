    How It Mixes Snake + CS

    Collect ammo crates to grow your snake body.

    T side: carry the bomb to site A (center), then hold E to plant.

    CT side: reach planted bomb and hold E to defuse.

    Colliding with any snake body or head duel kills both.

    Controls

    Arrow keys or WASD to move, hold E for plant/defuse.
